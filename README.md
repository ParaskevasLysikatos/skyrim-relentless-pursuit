# Relentless Pursuit

SKSE plugin for Skyrim SE/AE (built and tested against 1.6.1170).

- **Doors:** enemies in combat follow you through load doors. At startup the plugin switches on *Disable Combat Boundary* for every encounter zone, in memory only, so no plugin records are overridden (Open World Loot and other zone mods keep their level ranges).
- **Cells without a zone:** enemies won't follow you into a cell that has no encounter zone (most outdoor cells, many houses). While you're in one, it gets the no-boundary zone from `RelentlessPursuit.esp` (same approach as Enemies Follow Me).
- **Enemies come through after you:** the game itself only lets up to 2 enemies that are right at the door follow. When you use a load door, up to 8 enemies fighting you within 8192 units come through after you, each after about distance ÷ 350 seconds (2–12 s), at the spot where you came out, and only if you're still there; then they attack. Hostile enemies that are on alert but not fighting you yet (the rest of a camp that heard the fight) come too, after the ones fighting you (`bAlertedFollow`). Dragons, immobile and mounted enemies never come. Enemies in a quest alias come too unless `bSkipQuestActors = 1`.
- **They keep fighting:** enemies stop fighting once you leave their cell, so they come out calm and their normal AI would walk them straight back through the door. For 30 seconds after coming through (`fPursuitTime`), each one that isn't fighting attacks you again, and one that walks back through the door is brought out again, up to 2 times (`iMaxReturns`). After that the normal AI takes over.
- **Water:** every race that can swim loses *No Combat In Water*, so bandits, wolves, bears and so on swim after you. Races with no swim animation stop at the shore.
- **Door log:** every load-door use while enemies fight you is logged with their names and distance, each enemy that follows you through is logged, and a `Pursuit over:` line says what became of each one (still fighting you, killed, went back...).
- **Dragon guard:** dragons never follow you through a load door. When you use one, dragons fighting you stop combat. If one still comes through within 90 seconds, it is moved back outside that door.

`RelentlessPursuit.esp` is ESL-flagged (no full plugin slot) with one record and no masters. Safe to add mid-playthrough.

## Install (Mod Organizer 2)
Install `RelentlessPursuit.zip` as a mod, enable it and tick `RelentlessPursuit.esp` in the plugin list. Requires SKSE and Address Library for SKSE Plugins.

Settings: `SKSE/Plugins/RelentlessPursuit.ini`. Log: `Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/RelentlessPursuit.log`.

## Build
`package/RelentlessPursuit.esp` is generated by `tools/make_esp.py`.

GitHub Actions builds it on every push (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) with xmake and [CommonLibSSE-NG](https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR). Pushing a `v*` tag also publishes the zip as a release.
